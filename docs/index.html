Machine Learning for

A Crash-Course in Simulation-Based Inference

ICTP-SAIFR SAMMA 2026 PhD School — São Paulo, 2026 — Christoph Weniger (GRAPPA / University of Amsterdam)

  • Lecture 1a
    Machine Learning in Astroparticle Physics
    A guided tour of where ML has actually landed in APP/GW research, organised around the scientific-method loop: theory, simulator, instrument, data, inference. Closes on the two ways to do Bayesian inference with a simulator — setting up Lec 1b.
    Overview DINGO IceCube LIGO Emulators SBI taxonomy
  • Lecture 1b
    Approximate Bayesian Computation
    A three-demo staircase: rejection ABC → add a summary statistic → KDE in (θ, x)-space. Live, slider-driven demos expose the two failures — summary identification and high-D density estimation — that motivate the rest of the school.
    ABC Rejection sampling Summary statistics KDE Curse of dimensionality
  • Lecture 2a
    Linear Regression as Conditional Density Estimation
    Least-squares fitting is conditional density estimation: the curve is μ(x), the residual scatter is σ, together giving q(θ | x) = 𝒩(μ(x), σ²). Maximum likelihood, the closed-form solution, and where a fixed basis starts to overfit.
    Conditional densities Basis functions Maximum likelihood Closed-form solution Overfitting Validation
  • Lecture 2b
    Deep Neural Networks for Density Estimation
    The hand-picked basis was the bottleneck — so learn it. Neurons as learnable basis functions, the universal approximation theorem, training by gradient descent and mini-batch SGD, and how stacking layers builds richer effective bases.
    Neural networks Activations Universal approximation Gradient descent SGD Depth

    Session 1A — Train & Dissect an MLP (exercises) Session 1A — solutions

    Session 1B — Gravitational Wave NPE (exercises) Session 1B — solutions

  • Lecture 3a
    Making Deep Learning Work in Practice
    The training mechanics behind the MLP of Lecture 2b: making training work (early stopping, momentum, Adam, learning-rate schedules), making gradients flow (backpropagation, activations, initialisation, normalisation), and why overparameterised models still generalise (double descent, spectral bias).
    Backpropagation Gradient flow Initialisation Normalisation Adam Double descent Spectral bias
  • Lecture 3b
    Inference Heads and Parameter Models
    The anatomy of the SBI pipeline (simulator, inference loss, embedding); why minimising the NLL on simulated pairs recovers the posterior (neural posterior estimation); and the simplest inference head — the Gaussian head — together with where it breaks. Flows and continuous-time heads follow in Lecture 4.
    SBI pipeline NPE / NRE / NLE NLL minimisation Amortised inference Gaussian head Heteroscedastic noise
  • Lecture 4a
    Normalising Flows and Other Generative Models
    Beyond the Gaussian head: build expressive posteriors by warping a simple base distribution. Change of variables, the chain-of-Jacobians log-density, affine coupling layers and why their Jacobian is cheap, and conditioning a flow on the data — with a live 2D pushforward demo and APP/GW examples.
    Normalising flows Change of variables Coupling layers Jacobians Conditional flows Generative models
  • Lecture 4b
    Data Embedding with CNNs
    Turning raw, high-dimensional data into compact summaries the inference head can use. From the biology of visual perception to modern convnets: the convolution operation across channels, ReLU and max pooling, and the anatomy of a CNN (LeNet-5) as a learned embedding network for SBI.
    Embedding networks CNNs Convolution Pooling Classification LeNet-5
